Workshops

Our workshops bring students and researchers together for sustained, interdisciplinary inquiry into China and East Asia. Topics develop around the interests of participants.

Past session

Forests and the Politics of Environmental Transformation

Speaker
Weiran Zhu, UC Berkeley
Date
Thu, Oct 8, 2026

A discussion of forest destruction and afforestation in China, the organization of forestry work, and how local cases can reshape broader historical interpretations.

Discussion questions
  • What did it mean to conquer nature in a particular forestry project?
  • How did expertise, administrative priorities, and everyday needs interact?
  • What evidence connects local cases with broader historical claims?

Past session

Personal, Family, Lineage, and the State: Social Transformation in Twentieth-Century China and Family History Research

Speaker
Fan-chih Meng, Peking University
Date
Wed, Nov 25, 2026
Time
3–5 PM
Location
Seminar Room 341, East Asian Library · UC Berkeley

Beginning with the speaker’s Chinese translation of Joseph W. Esherick’s Ancestral Leaves, this lecture draws on more than two decades of research into the papers of the Chou family and on the archives of Chinese scholars overseas. It explores how private documents can reconstruct historical contexts and illuminate the interplay of individual, family, lineage, and state in twentieth-century China.

Upcoming session

From an Enemy to a Potential Partner: The P.R.C.’s Often-Overlooked Relationship with the U.S. (Oct. 1949–Apr. 1971)

Speaker
Boru Gao, UC Berkeley
Date
Thu, Dec 3, 2026
Time
7–9 PM
Location
Seminar Room 341, East Asian Library · UC Berkeley

A look at relations between the People’s Republic of China and the United States from the founding of the PRC in October 1949 to the ping-pong diplomacy of April 1971. The period is usually remembered for hostility and isolation; the discussion follows how the two countries moved from enemies toward potential partners well before rapprochement.

Book Talk with Liu Zongkun: Lives Remembered and Roads Traveled

Speaker
Liu Zongkun, Independent Attorney and Writer
Date
Mon, Dec 7, 2026
Time
2–4 PM
Location
Seminar Room 341, East Asian Library · UC Berkeley

A book talk on Liu Zongkun’s essay collection, published by 1 Plus Books in 2026. The essays revisit people he knew in China, met over two decades in Texas, and encountered while walking and cycling abroad. Their stories raise a question familiar to many who have moved away: what makes a place home?
