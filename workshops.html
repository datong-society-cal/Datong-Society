Workshops

Our workshops bring students and researchers together for sustained, interdisciplinary inquiry into China and East Asia. Topics develop around the interests of participants.

Fall 2026

2026-10-08 · Past session

Forests and the Politics of Environmental Transformation

Speaker: Weiran Zhu, UC Berkeley

A discussion of forest destruction and afforestation in China, the organization of forestry work, and how local cases can reshape broader historical interpretations.

Discussion questions
  • What did it mean to conquer nature in a particular forestry project?
  • How did expertise, administrative priorities, and everyday needs interact?
  • What evidence connects local cases with broader historical claims?
