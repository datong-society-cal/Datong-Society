Datong Society of China Studies · UC Berkeley

Collaboration合作交流

Ideas grow through conversation. We welcome thoughtful proposals from student organizations, scholars, cultural groups, and community partners whose work connects with China and East Asia.

交流是合作的起点。大同学社欢迎学生组织、学者、文化团体及社区伙伴提出与中国和东亚研究相关的合作构想。

Possible formats · 合作形式

  • Co-hosted events · 联合活动Talks, reading groups, film screenings, and interdisciplinary discussions.讲座、读书会、电影放映及跨学科讨论。
  • Guest conversations · 嘉宾对谈Open, substantive exchanges with scholars, writers, and practitioners.与学者、作者及各领域实践者开展开放而深入的交流。
  • Student-led projects · 学生项目Projects that bring different disciplines and communities into conversation.促进不同学科与社群对话的学生主导项目。

Who to contact · 联系方式

For collaboration proposals, email the Datong Society team. Please include a brief introduction, proposed format, possible dates, and the topic you hope to explore together.

合作提案请发送至学社邮箱。来信可简述团队背景、合作形式、拟议时间及希望共同讨论的主题。

partnerships@datong.studentorg.berkeley.edu ↗

Student organizers review proposals as their schedules allow. A proposal does not confirm an event or imply endorsement.

学社由学生运营，请留出讨论与回复时间；发送提案不代表活动已获确认或学社已作出背书。

如需参加活动或加入学社，请查看参与页面。
