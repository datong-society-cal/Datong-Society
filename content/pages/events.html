Past Events

Our archive includes public programs and other society activities since 2012.

The WeChat Lawsuit Against Trump — A Lecture by Keliang Zhu

Lecture2023 · 04 · 09

Attorney Keliang Zhu examined the origins, litigation, and legal strategies of the Chinese American community’s challenge to the 2020 WeChat ban, and considered the boundaries of the First Amendment in the digital age. Questions and discussion continued late into the evening.

Modern Chinese Embroidery on Pillow Covers

Exhibition2019 · Spring

Co-organized with the Sookmyung Women’s University Museum, this exhibition presented modern Chinese embroidered pillow covers to an overseas audience for the first time. Datong organized the exhibition checklist, volunteer schedules, loan agreements, and lecture posters; it was the society’s largest external program.

Still Life — Screening & Discussion

Film & Panel2015 · 2023

A screening of Jia Zhangke’s Venice Golden Lion-winning film. East Asian studies scholars offered commentary at the 2015 screening; a further screening was held in spring 2023 at members’ request.

The Chinese Mayor — Screening & Panel

Film & Panel2015 · 2021

A documentary about former Datong mayor Geng Yanbo’s urban redevelopment projects in Shanxi. Director Zhou Hao joined an online conversation in fall 2021. Datong prepared bilingual posters, the program, and the press release.

Author Talk & Book Signing — Geling Yan

Author Talk2023 · 10 · 22

The Chinese edition of Geling Yan’s latest novel, Milati, made its Bay Area debut. The talk was followed by a book signing and conversations with society members.

Global Studies 198 — Contemporary China

Student Course2016 – ongoing

A one-unit DeCal course sponsored by Professor Andrew F. Jones, chair of East Asian Languages and Cultures. Running across multiple academic years, from A History and Many Stories in 2020 to A History of Unfinished Tales in 2022, it is a central part of the society’s public academic programming.

Spring 2025 — Graduation Portraits

Community2025 · 05

Members marked graduation with portraits at Berkeley campus landmarks. The main photograph shows eleven graduates on the steps of Hearst Memorial Mining Building; other photographs feature Sather Tower, Doe Library, and other campus settings.

Fall 2018 — Salons & Member Portraits

Salon2018 · Fall

Salons on Chinese political thought, a film discussion of The Face of Chiang Kai-shek, society recruitment, and end-of-semester portraits.

Stanford Hoover Institution — Archive Visit

Field Trip~2017

A visit to Stanford’s Hoover Institution Archives to explore original materials from the Republican era and modern China.

Let’s Talk About Social Science — An Interdisciplinary Salon

Salon2016 · 09 · 22

Fifteen students gathered for ninety minutes of readings and questions on what makes good social science, helping establish Datong’s tradition of small discussion salons.

Annual Retreats & Senior Citations

Retreat · Citation2018 – 2019

Spring and fall retreats at the Marina and Santa Rosa, a Doe Library study group, and Senior Fellow thesis presentations by Amy Loo and Monica Liu.
