Upcoming · Book TalkWednesday, November 25, 2026

Translating Ancestral Leaves: A Family Journey through Chinese History

Fanzhi Meng (Peking University)
Time to be announced
Seminar Room 341, C. V. Starr East Asian Library · UC Berkeley

Fanzhi Meng will discuss his experience co-translating historian Joseph W. Esherick’s Ancestral Leaves: A Family Journey through Chinese History into Chinese. The book follows generations of the Ye family, tracing how political and social change shaped their lives from the late Qing through the Republican era and into the People’s Republic.

Upcoming · Book TalkMonday, December 7, 2026

Book Talk with Liu Zongkun: Lives Remembered and Roads Traveled

2–4 PM · Pacific Time
Seminar Room 341, C. V. Starr East Asian Library · UC Berkeley

Writer and Texas attorney Liu Zongkun discusses his essay collection, published by 1 Plus Books in 2026. The essays revisit people he knew in China, met over two decades in Texas, and encountered while walking and cycling abroad. Their stories raise a question familiar to many who have moved away: what makes a place home?
