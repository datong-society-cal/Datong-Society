01 / About the society

Where inquiry meets community 让研究与社群相遇

Datong Society is a student-led intellectual community connecting different ways of studying China and East Asia. We bring students and scholars together through reading, discussion, and public events.

大同学社以跨学科交流为起点，让研究中国与东亚的不同视角相遇。我们通过阅读、讨论与公共活动，连接学生、学者与更广泛的社群。
