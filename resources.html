Reading list: modern East Asian history

A systematic reading list on Chinese and Japanese history from the seventeenth through the twentieth century: the late imperial Qing, Tokugawa Japan, the crises of nineteenth-century China, the Meiji Restoration, Japan’s economic transformation, the Chinese revolution and early Republic, Meiji and Taishō politics, China under the Guomindang, Japanese imperialism and war, China under and after Mao, postwar Japan, and women in modern Japan. The books are well regarded in English-language scholarship, and several have received the John K. Fairbank Prize.
