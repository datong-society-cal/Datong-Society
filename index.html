Intro

Datong Society of China Studies at UC Berkeley is a student-led intellectual community bridging diverse academic perspectives on China and East Asia.

We welcome undergraduates, graduate students, and researchers across disciplines—ranging from history and cultural studies to political economy and empirical social science (both qualitative and quantitative).

  • Read Society Constitution (PDF)

Activities

Explore our upcoming seminars, reading groups, and guest lectures for the upcoming semester: Our core engagement formats include:

  • Discussion & Reading Groups: In-depth, text-based analytical discussions on classic literature and contemporary scholarship.
  • Seminars & Methodological Exchanges: Peer research presentations, discussion of works-in-progress, and interdisciplinary dialogues.
  • Scholar & Guest Exchanges: Occasional lectures and informal discussions with visiting scholars, writers, and cultural activist in the Bay Area.
  • Student-Led DeCal Courses: Student-taught curriculum. Offering as Hist 198 (Through the Camera: 20th-Century China in Transition) in Fall 2026.

About Us

We prioritize high-density intellectual exchange in an open, collaborative setting.

Whether you are pursuing formal China studies or applying social science methods to East Asian issues, Datong provides a space for critical inquiry and interdisciplinary learning.

朱可亮律师讲座：在美华人 vs 美国总统The WeChat Lawsuit Against Trump

Lecture2023 · 04 · 09

从 2020 年微信禁令谈起，律师朱可亮先生深入拆解华人社群诉总统行政令的起源、历程与法律策略，并反思第一修正案在数字时代的边界。讲座后 Q&A 持续到深夜。

韩国淑明女子大学汉字刺绣枕顶展Modern Chinese Embroidery on Pillow Covers

Exhibition2019 · Spring

与淑明女子大学博物馆联合办展——近代中国刺绣枕顶首次公开展出。配套展品清单、志愿者排班、借展协议与讲座海报均由大同学社独立执行，是社团史上规模最大的对外活动。

《中国市长》放映与座谈The Chinese Mayor — Screening & Panel
